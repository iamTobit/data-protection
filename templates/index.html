What SeraGuard Verifies

Our AI compliance engine checks your organization against the 8 statutory pillars enforced by the Data Commissioner:

Section 32 DPA

Consent & Burden of Proof

Consent must be unbundled, freely given, and with a simple withdrawal mechanism. Pre-ticked boxes are illegal in Kenya.

Section 37 DPA

Direct Marketing Opt-Out

Commercial use requires prior consent and a zero-cost opt-out facility. Over 60% of ODPC fines target unsolicited marketing.

Section 43 DPA

72-Hour Breach Reporting

Mandatory notification to the Data Commissioner within 72 hours of discovering any high-risk personal data breach.

Section 48 DPA

Cross-Border Cloud Transfers

Hosting data overseas (AWS, Azure, US servers) requires proof of adequate safeguards or Standard Contractual Clauses.
