2. Principles of Data Protection (Section 25, Act)

We process personal data strictly in accordance with the eight statutory principles under Section 25 of the Act:

  • Lawfulness, fairness and transparency: Processing is based on a lawful ground and disclosed to you.
  • Purpose limitation: Data is used only for the specific purposes set out in this notice.
  • Data minimisation: We collect only the data necessary for the intended purpose.
  • Accuracy: We take reasonable steps to keep data accurate and up-to-date.
  • Storage limitation: Data is retained for no longer than the periods described in Section 6.
  • Integrity and confidentiality: Appropriate technical and organisational safeguards are applied.
  • Accountability: We maintain records of processing activities and can demonstrate compliance.
  • Cross-border transfer protection: Any transfer outside Kenya complies with Sections 48 & 49.

7. Cross-Border Transfers Outside Kenya (Sections 48 & 49, Act)

To deliver our SaaS platform, we rely on cloud infrastructure and sub-processors that may operate outside Kenya:

  • Supabase (PostgreSQL): Managed cloud database hosting with encrypted storage.
  • Railway: Application hosting, container orchestration, and server infrastructure.
  • Groq / AI Inference Providers: Automated LLM text processing for statutory gap analysis.
  • Safaricom PLC: Domestic payment gateway for M-Pesa transactions within Kenya.

Statutory Safeguards: In compliance with Sections 48 and 49 of the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 and Part VII (Regulations 40–48) of the Data Protection (General) Regulations, 2021, all cross-border data transfers are governed by appropriate data protection safeguards and binding contractual agreements that mandate technical and organizational security safeguards compliant with Kenyan law, an adequacy decision, or explicit consent. Pursuant to Section 49(1), any cross-border transfer of sensitive personal data requires the explicit consent of the data subject and confirmation of appropriate safeguards or authorization by the Data Commissioner. Sub-processors are strictly prohibited from using customer data for secondary or independent purposes.

8. Security Safeguards & 72-Hour Breach Notification (Section 43, Act)

We implement a layered information security program, including:

  • Encryption: TLS 1.2+ for all data in transit; AES-256 encryption at rest.
  • Access Controls: Role-based access, multi-factor authentication for administrators, and strict Row-Level Security (RLS) on database tables.
  • Monitoring & Auditing: Automated intrusion detection, rate limiting, and routine security assessments.
  • Data Minimisation: Only authorised personnel can view personal data necessary for their support duties.

Breach Notification: If a personal-data breach occurs that is likely to result in a real risk of harm to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) within 72 hours of becoming aware under Section 43, and inform affected data subjects without undue delay.

10. Protection of Personal Data of Children (Section 33, Act)

Our software services are intended strictly for business owners, developers, legal professionals, and individuals aged 18 years and older. We do not knowingly collect or process personal data from children under 18. If we inadvertently receive data belonging to a child without verified parental or guardian consent, we will promptly delete it.
